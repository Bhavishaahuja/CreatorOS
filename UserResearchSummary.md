# User Research Summary
 
**Responses collected:** 7
**Method:** Google Form distributed to creator network
**Date:** June 2026
**Status:** Open — targeting 15–20 responses
 
---
 
## Who Responded
 
- 7/7 are Instagram creators — validates platform focus for MVP
- Follower breakdown: 4 under 10k, 1 at 10k–50k, 1 at 100k+, 1 under 1k
- 5/7 have a full-time job alongside creating
- 2/7 are students
- 0/7 create content full-time
---
 
## Key Findings
 
**1. Almost everyone is creating alongside a full-time commitment**
5 out of 7 have a full-time job. The remaining 2 are students. Nobody is creating full-time. This directly validates the core premise — the real obstacle is fitting content into a life that already has other priorities.
 
**2. Most creators don't plan at all**
3/7 plan content "in their head." 2/7 don't plan at all. Only 2/7 use a structured tool like Notion or a spreadsheet — and both of those are the creators with higher follower counts. Planning is informal, reactive, and undisciplined for the majority.
 
**3. Planning time is short, but posting frequency is low**
4/7 said planning takes under 30 minutes. But their posting frequency tells the real story — only 2/7 post 2–3x a week. The rest post once a week, once a month, or have stopped entirely. The problem isn't that planning takes too long. It's that without a structure, posting just doesn't happen consistently.
 
**4. The #1 fear about AI tools: losing their personal voice**
Every respondent who answered Q9 mentioned some version of the same concern — generic output, impersonal content, or ideas that don't feel like them. This came up in 5 out of 6 answered responses, unprompted.
 
**5. Usefulness of a schedule-aware tool: moderate-to-high**
Average rating: 3.6 / 5. Three respondents rated it 4/5, one rated it 5/5. The two lowest ratings (2/5) came from creators who are highly spontaneous and resist structured planning entirely — likely not the target user.

## Surprising Finding
 
Planning time is not the core pain point. Most creators said planning takes under 30 minutes. The real issue is that without external structure, planning just doesn't happen — content gets skipped or posted erratically. The problem CreatorOS solves is not saving planning time. It is giving creators a system they will actually follow because it fits around their real life.
 
This also reframes the product pitch: CreatorOS is less about speed and more about consistency through structure.
 
---
 
## Implication for Product
 
The biggest product risk is not whether the AI generates good ideas — it is whether the ideas feel personal. Every respondent who would not use an AI planning tool cited lack of personalisation as the reason. This means the approval flow (where users accept, reject, and add their own ideas) is not just a feature — it is the trust layer that makes the product viable. It must be prominent, fast, and feel like the user is in control.
